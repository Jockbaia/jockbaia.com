It’s hard to match the chaotic, erratic beauty of By Storm, the “double trio” rising from Injury Reserve’s ashes after Groggs’ passing in 2020. The grief that made their last project "By The Time I Get To Phoenix" so special is still haunting this album (and hurting my feelings in just the right way).
