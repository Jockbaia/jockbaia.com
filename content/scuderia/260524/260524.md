"Third World Man" is the closer of Gaucho and the last track from the classic era of Steely Dan discography before the 20 year hiatus. A song featuring a rich, layered instrumentation and an outro that, given the discography perspective, really sounds like a warm goodbye to the 70s. The rest of the album is just as legendary and the product of obsessive perfectionism: over a year in the studio and more than 40 different session musicians cutting up analog master tapes to stitch together this monster of a record.
