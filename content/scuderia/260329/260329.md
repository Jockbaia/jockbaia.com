Being a fan of Ye is probably the most toxic relationship I’ll ever have. He was the catalyst for my love of music and production, and I’ll always consider him one of the most talented artists of the century. Sadly, in recent years, his refusal to stay on a consistent medication regimen has led to a series of rants and tantrums that aren't backed by enough quality music to justify them. Occasionally, flashes of his former genius light up again, and it is these moments that I celebrate. 'ALL THE LOVE,' from his latest album Bully, is one of those special moments. It has that 'magic touch' that, despite everything, draws people back to him. A special mention must go to André Troutman’s talkbox contributions, truly a match made in heaven.
