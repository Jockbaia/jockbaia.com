"Born in the Echoes" from the Chemical Brothers is widely known for "Go", the track featuring Q-Tip that appeared in at least a thousand commercials in the last decade. Do yourself a favor and don't miss out the rest of the record. Ok, it's messy and all over the place, but I can assure you is packed with big beat bangers, with "Sometimes I Feel So Deserted" being a personal favorite.
