I am fully convinced that 'Il sogno di Maria' is, and will remain, the most beautiful italian song ever: a stunning narrative of the Archangel visiting the Virgin Mary, written by an author known more for his skepticism than his faith. That is exactly what makes the track a masterpiece: elevating a supernatural event into a raw, deeply human encounter.
