A special closing to a special album from Action Bronson. After 12 tracks packed with the usual bravado, the last track of "Planet Frog" finds Bronson flipping the scripting with one of the most introspective verses of its career. No punchlines, no fireworks, just a man coming to terms with time and his fear of becoming a stranger to the woman he loves.
