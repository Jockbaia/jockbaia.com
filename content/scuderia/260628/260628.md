I'm happy to announce that the wait for a proper Muse return to form seems to be finally over. Despite the over-the-top cliché-y grandeur, the new album from Muse is a banger from start to finish, thanks to a full return to the space rock genre they are known for. It's really hard to pick a favourite track, but the synth and guitar arpeggios in "Hexagons" make it a strong contender for the win.
