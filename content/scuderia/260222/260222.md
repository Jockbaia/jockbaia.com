This week we talk about "Telegraph Road", a tale about the rise and fall of American industry from the perspective of a Michigan road. A stunning masterpiece by Dire Straits, a band I was introduced to by my father when I was very young. The song’s architecture is perfect: the way it builds through recurring themes, the evocative storytelling in the lyrics, and, of course, Mark Knopfler in its prime: every element hits exactly as it should.
