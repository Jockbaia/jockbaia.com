The first BOC album in 13 years is a demanding first listen. It sounds to me like the evil brother of "Geogaddi", another album from the Scottish duo that I love. So many good tracks but I have to pick one: let's go with "Memory Death". Degraded synths, buried melodies and the raw, somehow nostalgic sound of a medical machine: a lesson in human fragility, and a reminder to have fun while we can as this game of life doesn’t last forever. Ok, maybe I'm just projecting too much.
