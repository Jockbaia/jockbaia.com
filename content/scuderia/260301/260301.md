The new Gorillaz album is out and it's a beauty. It gave me a sense of wonder that I was chasing since the release of Plastic Beach, a project that completely rewired my taste in music at 13 years old (let’s be honest: nobody listens to "Empire Ants" at that age and stays the same). This new album is a celebration of death and the strength needed to move forward, using traditional Hindu music as the foundation for the musical themes. Both Albarn and his Gorillaz co-creator, artist Jamie Hewlett, lost their fathers in 2024 and processed their grief with a trip to India. Albarn scattered his dad’s ashes in the river Ganges, embracing the country’s “more open and positive outlook on death”. The Sweet Prince is a heartfelt tribute to his father’s memory.
