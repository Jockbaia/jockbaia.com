This week we go 100% mainstream. My appreciation for some of Drake's music is no news at this point. I am not interested in the feud with Kendrick Lamar, but you are free to pick a side. As expected, the opening track addresses the beef: in "Make them cry" Drake talks about the aftermath of it and explains how it impacted his mental health. Call me naive, but I liked it. It's not his best work, but it's far from his worst (which lately seems to be the new standard).
