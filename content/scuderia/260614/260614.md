At this point I am pretty sure that Vince Staples will never make a bad album. The latest record "Cry Baby" is a big pivot in sound, EXTREMELY inspired from Paris Texas (which is a big plus in my house). Despite the rock sounding palette, the lyrics are some of the most fearless and confident from Vince: a critic stance on the current leadership leading the USA (as heavily suggested by the album cover and title). A culmination of the very best that West Coast Consciuous Hip Hop has to offer, with some good rap rock as the cherry on top.
