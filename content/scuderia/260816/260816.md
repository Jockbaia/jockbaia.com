I had the privilege of listening this song in a truly special setting, late at night somewhere deep in the Ligurian inland, in a small town far from the noise of civilisation, sung and played by dear friends of mine who I am lucky enough to call bandmates. A truly special moment, and a special song as well: in his final moments, a deer implores his hunter to show him kindness, willingly offering his corporeal remains in exchange for a last display of humanity.
