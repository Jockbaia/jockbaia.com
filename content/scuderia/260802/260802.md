I'm guilty of never having listened to The War On Drugs before. I found them by searching on Reddit for albums similar to "Pressure Machine" by The Killers, another record I love. This album hits the same spot, very Springsteen-esque. It peaks with "Suffering", more precisely on the second half of the track, which is one of my favourite discoveries of 2026. The piano on the outro is simply devastating (in a good way), and he rest of the album is really good as well!
