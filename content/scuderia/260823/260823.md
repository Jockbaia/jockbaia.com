It's impossible not to be in awe of KGATLW's creative output. Every single time they take something new out of the cylinder, and this time it may be one of the most unexpected moves ever: an acid house record, which is something you would not expect from the same band who made records like "Planet B" or the most recent "Petrodragonic Apocalypse". A permanent resident on my headphones for the rest of the summer. At this point no genre is off the table for their next album, so place your bets!
