"This Old Dog" is by far my favourite Mac DeMarco record. Deeply honest and heartfelt, with "Moonlight on the River" being no exception: a bittersweet message from Mac to an absent father and a reminder of what loving somebody that hurts us feels like. “It’s so strange deciding / how I feel about you”. The haunting outro perfectly encapsulate this feeling.
