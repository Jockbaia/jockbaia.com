Happy to share something that probably flew under the radar for 99.9% of people reading this: I discovered Deepho through a collaboration with Post Nebbia, another Italian act that I love, and found out that it was part of an album. Mairiso is awesome, full of experimental, gritty productions and angry, unfiltered lyricism. "luciano 2021" is my favorite, featuring a slow buildup of industrial elements.
