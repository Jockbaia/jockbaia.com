'Coi Capelli Sciolti Al Vento / Io Dirigo Il Tempo'. 'Il Giardino del Mago' ('The Wizard's Garden') is a hypnotic suite from Banco del Mutuo Soccorso's 1972 self-titled album. An ancestral 20-minute musical adventure with many twists and turns, sounding like an album within the album itself. A staple in Italian prog and one of my favourite tracks by B.D.M.S. (not to be confused with B.D.S.M.). One day I'll try to learn the organ solo starting at the 11:40 mark.
