Got curious and took the occasion of a new album to listen to some Harry Styles, an artist a friend of mine is very enthusiastic about (hi Jaya). A bit outside my usual rotation, but I have to admit that I really appreciated some of the cuts from it. 'Pop' is a first-listen standout, very Tame Impala-esque in both production and energy. Huge credits to the insane, plucky bass at the end of the track; it’s so loud that it fucks up the lows on my headphones.
