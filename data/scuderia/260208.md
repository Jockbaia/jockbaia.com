Everyone is talking about this "Marty Supreme" movie, so I bought a ticket. It was good: a beautiful and infuriating tale of an egotistical character in pursuit of a dream. However, at least for me, the soundtrack from Daniel Lopatin (aka Oneohtrix Point Never) completely stole the show. A masterclass on prog electronic that works both in the movie context and as a standalone album.
