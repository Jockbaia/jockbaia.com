I’m a huge fan of the blend of electronic and organic textures found throughout Bon Iver’s recent studio albums. I still remember hearing "22, A Million" for the first time back in 2016 and being completely absorbed by it. At that time, he shattered expectations trading his signature folk for glitchy textures and warped vocals. Yet lately, I keep returning to “S P E Y S I D E,” a track from the last album that strips everything away, leaving only an acoustic guitar and a devastating story of regret. A powerful return to the basics.
