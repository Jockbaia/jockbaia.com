Really enjoying the new EP from Nicolaj Serjotti, blending traditional hip-hop motifs with more experimental productions. Track 2, called "New York 2010", features a haunting, dreamlike childhood memory of Nicolaj watching a man fall from a skyscraper, over a beat that reminds me of something from early MIKE projects ("May God Bless Your Hustle" is the first album that comes to mind).
