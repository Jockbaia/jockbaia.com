"For the past 10 years, this album has been hand crafted with one intention: a personal challenge to myself to create my best work". With this introduction the curtain finally opens, revealing the first single from "The Fall Off", a project that J. Cole has teased since my early university years. The first single is a retrospective of his own life, reversing its narrative from the moment of his demise up to the womb. The result is immaculate: refined smooth production with lyrical density off the charts.
