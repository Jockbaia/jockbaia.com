As promised, I had to jump back into this new J. Cole record. With its ambitious length of 1 hour and 40 minutes (!), the record keeps the artist's promise to deliver a solid project as a closer to his career (though I'm pretty sure it won't be his last one). Lots of tracks to save but I'm really liking "Drum n Bass," with its laid-back storytelling and a beat reminiscent of the ones I heard on "2014 Forest Hills Drive" years ago.
