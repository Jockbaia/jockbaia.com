"Love in a time of chaos". James Blake's latest album is a powerful record, from the vocals to the production to the songwriting. The occurrence of someone with the talent to do all this with such consistency is not usual, so let's cherish it. Many beautiful cuts on this one, so it's really difficult to pick one. I'll go with "Didn't Come To Argue": love the sudden style changeup and the lush vocals that precede it.
